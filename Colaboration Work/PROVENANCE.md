# Provenance matrix

Each team member has their own rows. A row says what you contributed, where it came from, one check you ran that could have failed, and what happened. "Looked fine" does not count. A good check has a number or a pass or fail outcome that someone else could repeat.

How to use it: replace the bracketed parts, run the check, and write the real result and date. Add more rows for anything else you made or checked. Every team member needs at least one row, including the fourth member.
